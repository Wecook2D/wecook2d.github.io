Chappo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Chappo is a mobile game published by wecook2d. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android and iOS versions.

In short

Chappo does not ask you to create an account, and does not collect your name, email address or location. Your game progress is saved on your own device. Three Google services receive data: AdMob, which shows the ad you may choose to watch to continue a run; Firebase, which reports crashes and counts how the game is played; and Google Play Games (or Apple Game Center on iOS), which holds your scores, achievements and cloud saves.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time it starts. Until you accept, none of the data in the table below is collected — the advertising SDK is not even started, and analytics and crash reporting stay switched off. You can change your answer at any time from Options → Privacy options inside the game.

Data not collected

Chappo does not collect your name, email address, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, audio, files, calendar, browsing history, or your precise location. The game requests no permission for any of these. It contains no in-app purchases.

Your local progress file (playerGameData) stays on your device and is never sent to us; if you are signed in to Play Games, a copy is stored in your own Google account so it can follow you to another device. We operate no server of our own and hold no database of players.

Your choices

  • Consent — in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent message on first launch. Refusing it means no ad is ever requested, and no analytics or crash data is sent. The game itself remains fully playable, and the offer to watch an ad to continue a run simply never appears.
  • Changing your mind — Options → Privacy options reopens that same screen, whichever way you answered.
  • Advertising ID — it can be reset or deleted from your device's privacy settings, on any device, anywhere.

Children

Chappo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Your rights

wecook2d is based in France, so if you are in the European Economic Area the GDPR gives you the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to object to its processing, to withdraw a consent you have given, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

Deleting your data

Since Chappo keeps no data of its own on any server, deletion is handled where the data actually lives:

  • Local progress — uninstalling the game removes it from your device.
  • Scores, achievements and cloud saves — delete them from your Google Play Games account settings, or your Apple Game Center settings on iOS.
  • Advertising identifier — reset or delete it in your device's privacy settings.
  • Analytics and crash data — withdrawing your consent from Options → Privacy options stops any further collection; what was already sent is held by Google under its own retention rules, and uninstalling the game breaks the link with your device.

You can also write to chappo.runner@gmail.com to request deletion, and we will help you carry it out.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting data differently — for instance if we add a new advertising provider or in-app purchases — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
