Chappo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Chappo is a mobile game published by wecook2d. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android and iOS versions.

In short

Chappo does not ask you to create an account, and does not collect your name, email address or location. Your game progress is saved on your own device. Two third-party services do receive data: an advertising service, which uses your device's advertising identifier to show ads, and Google Play Games (or Apple Game Center), which stores your scores and achievements if you choose to sign in.

Data collected

DataCollected byPurposeCan you refuse?
Advertising identifier and device identifiers Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), a third party Displaying ads, measuring their performance, and detecting advertising fraud Not from inside the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
Player account identifier, scores, achievements, saved games Google Play Games Services (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS) Leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves Yes. Sign-in is optional and the game is fully playable signed out; you can sign out at any time from the main screen.

The advertising identifier is shared with a third party. The data handled by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center is processed by those platforms on our behalf so the game can offer leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves.

Data not collected

Chappo does not collect your name, email address, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, audio, files, calendar, browsing history, or your location. The game requests no permission for any of these. It contains no in-app purchases and no analytics or crash-reporting service.

Your local progress file (playerGameData) stays on your device and is never sent to us. We operate no server of our own and hold no database of players.

Third-party services

These services have their own privacy policies, which govern what they do with the data they receive:

  • Unity Ads — Unity game player and app user privacy policy
  • Google Play Games Services — Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Game Center (iOS) — Apple Privacy Policy

Children

Chappo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Your rights

wecook2d is based in France, so if you are in the European Economic Area the GDPR gives you the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

Deleting your data

Since Chappo keeps no data of its own on any server, deletion is handled where the data actually lives:

  • Local progress — uninstalling the game removes it from your device.
  • Scores, achievements and cloud saves — delete them from your Google Play Games account settings, or your Apple Game Center settings on iOS.
  • Advertising identifier — reset or delete it in your device's privacy settings.

You can also write to chappo.runner@gmail.com to request deletion, and we will help you carry it out.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting data differently — for instance if we add a new advertising provider or a crash-reporting service — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
