Our first game

Chappo

Run, jump, switch colour. Chain combos, keep the streak. Just one more run.

Chappo runs on his own. Your job is to jump at the right moment and switch between red and blue to grab the right items and get through what's in the way. Two zones on screen, one thumb, off you go.

  • Two colours, one rule, three seconds to get it, then it's all reflexes.
  • Chain items of the same colour, fill the gauge and trigger Fever.
  • Bosses block the way and bonus stages reward the bold.
  • No lives, no energy, no waiting. You lose, you go again.
